Replacement guide

When is replacement necessary?

Not all windscreen damage can be stabilised with resin. Replacement may be the appropriate route when visibility or structural integrity is affected.

  • Long or spreading cracks
  • Damage in the driver's direct line of sight
  • Impact points at the windscreen edge
  • Multiple or complex damaged areas
