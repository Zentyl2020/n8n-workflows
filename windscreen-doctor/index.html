Repair or replace?

We will help you make the call.

The final decision requires an assessment, but these common indicators provide a useful starting point.

Repair may be suitable when:

The damage is small, isolated and away from critical areas.

  • The chip is relatively small.
  • It is outside the driver's direct line of sight.
  • The damage has not reached the windscreen edge.
  • There are no long cracks spreading from it.
OR

Replacement may be needed when:

The damage affects visibility or the windscreen's integrity.

  • There is a long or spreading crack.
  • The damage obstructs the driver's vision.
  • A chip or crack reaches the outer edge.
  • There are multiple or complex impact points.

Not sure? Send a clear photo. We will assess it before recommending a service.
