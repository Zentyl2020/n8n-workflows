# Windscreen Doctor — Google Stitch Design Brief

## Project

Design a polished, conversion-focused website variation for **Windscreen
Doctor**, a nationwide South African mobile windscreen service.

The company offers two core services:

1. Windscreen chip repair
2. Full windscreen replacement

The main differentiator is mobile fitting: **the technician comes to the
customer's home or workplace**.

Create an original visual variation rather than copying an existing
automotive-glass website. The result should feel trustworthy, modern,
straightforward and distinctly South African.

## Primary goal

Encourage visitors to:

1. Send a photograph of the damage
2. Provide their vehicle and location details
3. Request a quotation

The design should make those actions obvious within the first screen.

## Audience

- South African private vehicle owners
- Drivers with a chipped, cracked or smashed windscreen
- Busy customers who prefer service at home or work
- Cash-paying customers
- Customers who may be unsure whether their insurance provides glass cover

Users may arrive stressed, on a mobile phone and looking for quick guidance.
Keep the experience calm and uncomplicated.

## Brand positioning

**Core promise:** Chipped or cracked windscreen? We come to you.

**Primary tagline:** Clearly better. We come to you.

**Brand personality:**

- Helpful
- Competent
- Reassuring
- Direct
- Convenient
- Safety conscious
- Modern, but not overly technical

Avoid making the brand resemble a medical clinic. Do not use a red medical
cross.

## Visual identity

### Colour palette

| Token | Colour | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Midnight Navy | `#092E3E` | Trust, header, footer and dark sections |
| Clear Teal | `#00A6A6` | Brand accent, links, icons and clarity |
| Action Amber | `#F6A800` | Primary quotation and booking actions |
| Sky Mist | `#EAF7F6` | Soft section backgrounds |
| Graphite | `#17242C` | Main body copy |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Main background and reversed copy |

Use amber sparingly for important conversion actions. Use navy text on amber
buttons. Avoid white body text on bright teal where the contrast is too low.

### Typography

- Headings: **Sora**, weight 700–800
- Body and interface: **Source Sans 3**, weight 400–700

Use large, confident headings and short paragraphs. Body copy must remain
comfortable to read on smaller mobile screens.

### Logo direction

Use a simple wordmark reading:

**WINDSCREEN**  
**DOCTOR**

Pair it with an original icon combining:

- a vehicle windscreen outline
- a subtle shield shape
- a four-point glass-reflection or clarity sparkle

The logo must work on a website header, mobile van, technician shirt and
WhatsApp profile image.

## Suggested design variation

Create a premium, editorial automotive variation with:

- strong asymmetrical hero composition
- dark navy hero background
- authentic technician photography
- glass-reflection shapes or subtle translucent panels
- generous white space
- restrained rounded corners between 10px and 20px
- thin teal line icons
- amber conversion buttons
- clear modular cards
- a visible mobile quotation action

Avoid generic corporate gradients, excessive glassmorphism, tyre-track
graphics, checkered racing patterns and aggressive red automotive styling.

## Navigation

Desktop navigation:

- Home
- Chip Repair
- Windscreen Replacement
- Mobile Fitment
- How It Works
- Areas We Serve
- FAQs
- Get a Quote

The **Get a Quote** item should be an amber button.

Mobile navigation:

- compact logo
- hamburger menu
- persistent bottom actions:
  - Send a Photo
  - Get a Quote

## Homepage structure

### 1. Utility bar

Short message:

> Nationwide mobile service across South Africa

Optional secondary action:

> Request an assessment

### 2. Header

Include the Windscreen Doctor logo, navigation and quotation button.

Keep the header sticky without taking excessive vertical space.

### 3. Hero

**Eyebrow:**

> Mobile windscreen specialists

**Headline:**

> Chipped or cracked? We come to you.

**Supporting copy:**

> Mobile windscreen chip repair and full windscreen replacement at your home
> or workplace, across South Africa.

**Primary action:**

> Send a Photo

**Secondary action:**

> Get a Quote

Include three short reassurance points:

- Straightforward assessment
- All major vehicle makes
- Home or workplace visits

Hero photography should feature a professional South African technician
working on a modern vehicle at a customer's home or workplace. Leave clear
negative space for the headline.

### 4. Service reassurance strip

Create four concise items:

- Mobile service — We come to you
- Chip repair — Repair where suitable
- Full replacement — When repair is not suitable
- Nationwide coverage — Across South Africa

### 5. Two service choices

#### Windscreen chip repair

> Address suitable stone chips before they spread. Repair retains the
> original windscreen and is generally the quicker, more economical route.

Action:

> Send a chip photo

#### Full windscreen replacement

> For severe cracks, edge damage and damage that cannot be safely repaired,
> we arrange a complete mobile windscreen replacement.

Action:

> Request a replacement quote

Make the two options visually distinct and easy to compare.

### 6. Mobile fitting feature

**Heading:**

> Your car can stay where it is.

**Copy:**

> A damaged windscreen is disruptive enough. Our mobile model is designed so
> you do not need to organise lifts, sit in traffic or lose half your day at
> a fitment centre.

Feature points:

- Choose a convenient location
- Share the details in advance
- Know the plan before the visit

Action:

> Arrange Mobile Service

### 7. Repair-or-replace guide

Create a simple two-column comparison.

#### Repair may be suitable when:

- The chip is relatively small
- It is outside the driver's direct line of sight
- The damage has not reached the windscreen edge
- There are no long cracks spreading from it

#### Replacement may be needed when:

- There is a long or spreading crack
- Damage obstructs the driver's vision
- A chip or crack reaches the outer edge
- There are multiple or complex impact points

Include this disclaimer:

> The final decision requires an assessment. Send a clear photo and we will
> help determine the appropriate next step.

### 8. How it works

Use a four-step visual process:

1. **Send a photo**  
   Share an image of the damage and where it sits on the windscreen.

2. **Add vehicle details**  
   Provide the year, make, model and vehicle location.

3. **Review the quote**  
   Receive the proposed service, price and appointment options.

4. **We come to you**  
   A mobile technician attends the agreed location.

### 9. Nationwide coverage

**Heading:**

> One mobile service, wherever your road takes you.

Include a stylised South African map and initial city examples:

- Johannesburg
- Pretoria
- Cape Town
- Durban
- Gqeberha
- Bloemfontein

Prompt:

> Tell us your suburb and we will confirm mobile-service availability.

Do not imply that every remote location is immediately available.

### 10. Reasons to choose Windscreen Doctor

Create six compact benefit cards:

- Repair-first thinking
- Mobile by design
- Clear recommendations
- Focused windscreen expertise
- Respect for the customer's time
- Nationwide ambition

Do not invent customer numbers, ratings, qualifications or guarantees.

### 11. FAQs

Include accordion questions:

1. Can my windscreen chip be repaired?
2. Do you come to my home or workplace?
3. How much does repair or replacement cost?
4. Can I claim through my vehicle insurance?
5. What if my vehicle has cameras or driver-assistance features?
6. Can I drive immediately after the work?

Insurance wording:

> Insurance-claim support is currently being finalised. Cover differs between
> policies, so customers should confirm their glass cover and excess with
> their insurer.

ADAS wording:

> Some vehicles require camera recalibration after windscreen replacement.
> The requirement must be identified from the vehicle details before booking.

Do not claim that Windscreen Doctor performs ADAS recalibration until this is
confirmed.

### 12. Quotation section

**Heading:**

> Show us what happened.

Fields:

- Customer name
- Mobile number
- Province
- City or suburb
- Vehicle year
- Vehicle make
- Vehicle model
- Type of damage
- Photograph upload
- Additional notes
- Privacy consent

Primary action:

> Request My Quote

Also show a prominent WhatsApp alternative once the business number is
available.

### 13. Footer

Include:

- logo and tagline
- service links
- areas served
- FAQs
- quotation link
- placeholder contact details
- privacy policy
- terms
- POPIA notice

## Image direction

Prioritise authentic photography showing:

- a South African technician
- diverse South African customers
- a technician repairing a chip
- a technician replacing a windscreen
- mobile service at a suburban home
- mobile service in a workplace parking area
- close-up detail of windscreen damage
- recognisable but subtle South African environments

Photography should feel bright, capable and human. Avoid visibly American or
European road signs, left-hand-drive vehicles, snow, foreign registration
plates and generic workshop-only imagery.

## Responsive requirements

Create both:

1. Desktop design at approximately 1440px wide
2. Mobile design at approximately 390px wide

Mobile priorities:

- headline and service promise visible immediately
- large thumb-friendly actions
- quotation flow with one field per row
- no horizontal scrolling
- compact sticky header
- persistent Send a Photo and Get a Quote controls
- accordions for longer informational content

## Accessibility requirements

- Meet WCAG AA colour contrast
- Use visible keyboard focus states
- Use semantic heading hierarchy
- Keep body text at least 16px
- Provide descriptive image alt text
- Do not communicate repair/replacement status through colour alone
- Ensure all inputs have visible labels
- Respect reduced-motion preferences

## Content and legal constraints

Do not make unconfirmed claims about:

- insurance-company partnerships
- claim processing
- same-day or emergency service
- fixed response times
- SABS certification
- technician accreditation
- workmanship warranties
- lifetime guarantees
- ADAS recalibration capability
- fixed prices
- customer ratings or review counts

These items can be added after the business confirms them.

Use South African terminology:

- windscreen, not windshield
- mobile number, not cell-phone number
- quotation or quote
- suburb and province
- bakkie where relevant

## Desired Stitch output

Produce:

1. One complete high-fidelity desktop homepage
2. One complete high-fidelity mobile homepage
3. Reusable header, button, service-card, FAQ and form components
4. A small design-token reference showing colours, type and spacing
5. A clearly different visual variation while preserving the supplied brand
   palette, typography, content and conversion journey

The final design should be ready to recreate as a responsive WordPress
website.
