Repairability guide

Is the chip repairable?

The size, depth and position all matter. A photograph gives us a useful starting point, but the final decision requires assessment.

Request an Assessment

Repair may be suitable

  • The chip is relatively small
  • It is outside the driver's direct line of sight
  • The damage has not reached the edge
  • There are no long cracks

Replacement may be needed

  • A crack extends across the windscreen
  • Damage obstructs the driver's vision
  • The impact reaches an outer edge
  • There are multiple complex impacts
