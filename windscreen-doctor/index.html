Damage is the reason to replace. Not a menu of options.

We specialise in windscreen replacement. A chip, several chips, a spreading crack or shattered glass are conditions that typically mean new glass — not a DIY wait-and-see.

Chips

One chip can spread. Several chips usually mean replacement.

Replacement is commonly the right call where:

  • There are multiple impact points
  • A chip sits in the driver’s view
  • The damage is near the edge
  • A crack has started from the chip

Cracks and shattered glass

If the glass is compromised, replace it.

Replacement is needed where:

  • The crack is long or spreading
  • Damage reaches the windscreen edge
  • Visibility is no longer safe
  • The screen is shattered or starred across

Glass is part of the vehicle.

A modern windscreen is not only a window. It can interact with cameras, sensors, rain and light systems, driver-assistance technology and the vehicle structure.

  • Cameras and sensorsSome vehicles carry forward-facing cameras in the glass or mirror housing.
  • Rain and light systemsReplacement may affect sensors that sit on or behind the windscreen.
  • Vehicle structureThe windscreen contributes to cabin strength. Fitment and curing matter.

Some vehicles may require calibration or additional procedures after replacement. We’ll advise where this applies. Windscreen Doctor does not currently claim to perform ADAS calibration in-house.

Correct fitment

The glass specification depends on the vehicle year, make, model and fitted features. Those details come first.

Proper preparation

Trim, bonding surfaces and the opening need to be prepared before new glass is set.

Quality glass

The windscreen is matched to the vehicle. Confirm the exact glass grade with the quotation — this prototype does not publish unverified OEM or certification claims.

Safe curing

After replacement, follow the technician’s safe-drive-away guidance. Conditions affect curing time.

Clean finishing

The job should leave the vehicle usable and presentable, with aftercare explained before the technician leaves.
